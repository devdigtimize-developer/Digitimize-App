# DIGTIMIZE Website Content Strategy

**Prepared for:** Digtimize website refresh  
**Role of this document:** Brand voice, page audits, ready-to-use copy, service messaging, FAQs, placeholders, and implementation checklist  
**Rule:** Do not invent founder details, team identities, client results, awards, pricing guarantees, or unverified metrics.

---

## 1. Brand Voice and Messaging Guidelines

### Brand personality
Digtimize sounds like a **calm, capable digital partner** — modern, clear, and human. We explain technology without talking down to anyone, and without hiding behind jargon.

### Writing style
- Short paragraphs (2–4 lines)
- Concrete verbs: build, connect, follow up, launch, improve
- Benefits before tools
- One idea per sentence when possible
- Headlines that answer “what do I get?” not “how clever are we?”

### Tone of voice
- Professional, not corporate
- Confident, not loud
- Helpful, not salesy
- Premium, not exclusive

### Words to use
- clear next step, goals, customers, leads, website, store, app, automation, systems, support, plan, launch, grow

### Words to avoid (unless the audience is clearly technical)
- RAG, LLM, stack, sprints, wireframes (prefer “layout drafts”), pipelines (prefer “lead stages”), headcount, deliverables-as-jargon, “best in the world,” “guaranteed results,” “#1 agency”

### Guidelines for non-technical customers
1. Name the **business problem** first.
2. Explain the solution in **everyday language**.
3. State the **benefit** (time saved, clearer process, more enquiries, easier selling).
4. Mention tools only as supporting detail.
5. End with a **gentle CTA**.

## 6. Customer-Friendly FAQ Content

**Q: What does Digtimize do?**  
A: We help businesses build websites, online stores, mobile apps, automation systems (including GoHighLevel), practical AI tools, and custom software — so your digital setup supports real growth.

**Q: Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?**  
A: No. You bring the business goals. We translate them into a clear plan and handle the technical work.

**Q: Who is Digtimize for?**  
A: Business owners, growing teams, and marketing agencies that need a reliable partner for websites, stores, apps, and automation.

**Q: What happens after I contact you?**  
A: We review what you share, then reply with a clear next step — usually a short discovery conversation and, when it fits, a simple proposal.

**Q: How long does a project take?**  
A: It depends on the scope. After we understand your goals, you get a realistic timeline before work starts. We do not begin without a clear plan.

**Q: How does pricing work?**  
A: We discuss scope first, then share clear pricing for that plan. Platform subscriptions (such as Shopify or GoHighLevel) are separate and explained upfront. *[Confirm exact pricing model with Digtimize before publishing fixed prices.]*

**Q: Will you still help after launch?**  
A: Yes, when support is part of the agreement — for fixes, improvements, and ongoing guidance.

**Q: Can you work with tools we already use?**  
A: Often yes. Tell us what you already run. We recommend what fits — not unnecessary rebuilds.

**Q: Why should I trust Digtimize?**  
A: Direct communication with the people doing the work, a clear plan before build, and systems designed to keep working after launch. *[Add verified projects and testimonials when available.]*

## 9. Homepage Section Order and UX Review

### Existing Home page section order (before this UX pass)

1. **Hero** — rotating value propositions  
2. **Shift / problem comparison** — disconnected tools vs Digtimize path  
3. **Services overview** — eight service cards  
4. **Connected capabilities** — interactive “stack” diagram  
5. **Stats counter** — soft capability metrics  
6. **Selected clients** — logo strip (“Trusted by teams worldwide”)  
7. **Case studies** — project carousel  
8. **Why Digtimize (platforms)** — tool cards (second “Why Digtimize”)  
9. **Why Digtimize (benefits)** — dark trust/benefits block  
10. **Process** — four steps  
11. **Tech orbit** — tools visualization  
12. **FAQ teaser**  
13. **Footer** (global layout)

### Purpose of each section (journey role)

| Section | Ideal role in journey |
|---------|------------------------|
| Hero | Understand what Digtimize does |
| Shift | Recognize the business problem |
| Services | See solutions |
| Stats | Soft credibility |
| Why Digtimize benefits | Trust / differentiation |
| Process | Understand how engagement works |
| Case studies | Proof / relevance |
| Tech | Optional depth for curious visitors |
| FAQ | Reduce friction |
| Final CTA | Convert |
| Footer | Navigation + contact |

### Content and UX problems found

1. **Journey was out of order** — Case studies and client logos appeared *before* “why Digtimize” and *before* process, so visitors saw proof before understanding benefits and how work happens.  
2. **Duplicate “Why Digtimize”** — platforms block + dark benefits block competed and diluted the message.  
3. **Triple capability messaging** — Connected Capabilities + Platforms + Tech Orbit repeated “what we work with” three times and were too technical mid-page.  
4. **No final homepage CTA** — FAQ ended the page; conversion relied on nav only.  
5. **Selected clients** claimed “Trusted by teams worldwide” with logos that are not verified client relationships in this codebase — trust risk for non-technical visitors.  
6. **Case studies headline** (“Real Work. Measureable Results.”) over-claimed and contained a typo.  
7. **Connected Capabilities** (“How Digtimize connects the stack”) is strong for technical audiences but interrupts non-technical flow right after services.

### Recommended (and implemented) section order

1. Hero  
2. Shift / problem → solution  
3. Services overview  
4. Connected Capabilities (restored)  
5. Stats counter (verified soft stats only)  
6. Why Digtimize (benefits / after launch)  
7. How we work / process (+ link to full Work roadmap)  
8. Case studies  
9. Tools we use (tech orbit — moved later)  
10. FAQ teaser  
11. Final CTA (`CTASection`)  
12. Footer (global)

### Reason for every major reordering decision

| Change | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| Keep Shift immediately after Hero | Problem recognition right after value prop is classic conversion flow |
| Services before trust/process | Visitors need “what can you do for me?” before “why you / how” |
| Stats after services | Soft proof after solutions, not buried after diagrams |
| Single Why Digtimize block before Process | One clear benefits chapter before “how we work” |
| Process before Case studies | Understand the engagement model before reviewing projects |
| Tech after Case studies | Tools are supporting detail, not the main story |
| FAQ then CTA | Answer doubts, then ask for contact |
| Restore Connected Capabilities after Services | Unique visual “how we connect systems” section; kept because it is a core Home experience |
| Remove Platforms “Why Digtimize” from Home | Removed duplicate Why Digtimize + tool pitch |
| Remove Selected Clients from Home | Unverified logos / “worldwide” claim; restore when real logos are provided |
| Add final CTA | Completes the conversion journey |

### Content improvements made in this pass

- Softened case studies heading to: **“Project stories from our work.”**  
- Tech section reframed as **“Tools we use”** with plain-language support copy  
- Process section adds link: **See the full project roadmap** → `/work`  
- Final CTA uses existing `CTASection` copy (“Tell us about your business”)  
- Preserved earlier plain-language copy on Hero, Shift, Services, FAQ

### Missing sections (intentionally not invented)

- **Real testimonials** — do not add until verified quotes exist  
- **Verified client logo strip** — reinstate Selected Clients only with approved logos  
- **Founder intro on Home** — optional later; About/Founder already cover this without cluttering Home  
- **Connected Capabilities on Home** — restored after Services (interactive stack diagram). Keep copy in plain language; do not treat it as a replacement for the services grid.

### Final homepage conversion journey

**Understand → Feel the problem → See solutions → How systems connect → Soft proof → Why Digtimize → How we work → See work → Optional tools → Answer doubts → Take action**
